Fitness Test Pro Elite

Score any military fitness test in seconds.

Health & Fitness · iPhone · Version 3.5.3

View on the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Fitness Test Pro Elite is a fitness test calculator for the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard and special operations screening tests. Pick your branch, enter your events and see your total and pass/fail using the published scoring tables.

Branches and tests

  • Army: Army Fitness Test (AFT), alternate aerobic events, Combat or General standard by MOS, Combat Field Test timer, waist-to-height check
  • Navy: Physical Readiness Test with altitude adjustments, Navy Combat Fitness Test, body composition
  • Air Force: PFRA with the 2026 scoring charts, waist-to-height component, special warfare standards
  • Marine Corps: PFT and CFT, class thresholds, body composition
  • Coast Guard: PFT with run, row or swim, body composition
  • Special operations: selection-course entry tests such as the SEAL and SWCC PST, Air Force special warfare, MARSOC, Ranger and Special Forces screeners

iPhone Duo

On the open inner display, the menu sits on the left page and the calculator on the right, with your live score next to your inputs.

Pricing

Free to score a test in every branch. Ads appear on result screens.

The Full Version is a one-time purchase per branch, or All Branches unlocks every branch, including branches added later. Any purchase removes ads. No subscription.

Full Version adds saved results and history, event-by-event breakdowns, target and goal planners, and branch extras like combat fitness tests, body composition, scoring tables and guided timers.

Help and FAQ

Is this an official app?

No. Fitness Test Pro Elite is an independent, unofficial calculator. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Department of Defense, Department of Homeland Security or any government agency. Official results come from your command's fitness program and current policy.

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap Restore Purchases in the paywall or in About, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I remove ads?

Buy any branch or All Branches.

Where are my results saved?

On your device only. No login, no account, no cloud sync.
