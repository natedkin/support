Jumpin Ninja!

Tap to kick. Hold to slow time. Climb.

Action game · iPhone · Version 1.7

View on the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

A one-tap wall-kick climber. Kick between walls, juke in mid-air, and hold to enter Focus, slowing time to thread the gap before the rising blades catch you.

Highlights

  • Four worlds: Mountain Gorge, Bamboo Grove, Castle Siege and Neon City, each with its own hazards
  • Focus (slow time) for clean, cinematic dodges
  • Upgrade your Dojo, unlock ninjas, take on missions and earn belts
  • Collect shuriken and unlock outfits and trails
  • Progress follows you to a new iPhone through iCloud

Pricing

Free to play. A Market with optional reward videos, never forced. Optional one-time purchases:

  • Ninja Pack (every skin)
  • Starter Bundle
  • Shuriken Doubler
  • Dojo Patron (reward bonuses with no video, plus the Patron outfit)
  • Storm Clan and Blossom Clan outfit sets

No subscription.

Help and FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore Purchases in the shop with the same Apple Account.

Does it play offline?

Yes. Reward videos need a connection.

Found a bug?

Email support and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
