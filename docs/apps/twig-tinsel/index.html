Twig & Tinsel: Christmas Tree

Decorate a Christmas tree where every bough bends.

Games · iPhone · Version 1.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Hang an ornament and the branch dips, sways and settles. Hang a heavy brass bell on one side and the whole tree starts to lean. Trim it to the brief, keep it standing straight, then plug in the lights.

What's inside

  • 24 decorating briefs from 8 households, each read out loud
  • Glass, felt, wood, brass and keepsake ornaments, each with its own weight
  • Lights and garlands you drag end to end between two hooks
  • Free Trim: decorate any tree with no brief, save it and keep a photo card
  • The Advent Box: a new ornament every day from December 1 to 24
  • Both Sides: dress the front and the back of one tree
  • Winter Lodge from December 26: a birch, a lodge pine and six winter briefs

iPhone Duo

Held like a book, the brief and the ornament box sit on one page and the tree on the other. Half folded on a table, the tree stands up and the box lies flat. Open flat, two people can trim Both Sides face to face.

Pricing

Free to play: the first brief from every household, Free Trim on the noble fir, Both Sides, the whole Advent Box and photo cards.

The Full Tree is a one-time purchase that adds the other 16 briefs, the 6 Winter Lodge briefs, every tree, every ornament family, all lights, garlands and toppers, six save shelves and twinkle clips. No subscription and no ads.

Help and FAQ

Can ornaments fall off?

No. An overloaded bough lets the piece slide down to a lower bough, and if nothing can hold it, it goes back to the box.

Why did my lights go dark?

A strand pulled tighter than its length unplugs. Move one end to a closer hook.

I missed a day of the Advent Box.

Nothing is lost. Every day that has passed opens when you visit, and opened pieces stay in your box.

How do I restore the Full Tree?

Tap Restore on the Full Tree panel or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Settings > Replay tutorial.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
