Pointy Stick

Carry it, stack it, build the drones.

Casual game · iPhone · Version 2.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Run a scrappy drone startup out of a cramped office. Grab props, batteries and frames off the shelves and they stack up on your back. Carry them to the assembly bench, watch it build a drone, and scoop up the cash. Spend it on floor pads to open new rooms, shelves, benches and bigger carry loads.

Highlights

  • Carry and stack: the taller your stack, the more you earn, and the more it sways
  • Watch the corners: sharp turns and bumping into busy coworkers on coffee runs spill parts off the top
  • Turn finished drones into a crew that flies parts around the office for you
  • Test flights: every few drones, time each launch against the gusts for a bonus
  • Unlock new floors, benches, walk and build speed upgrades
  • iPhone Duo: on the open inner display, an Order Board sits beside a wider office view; in tabletop, the office is on top and the controls are on the bottom

Pricing

Free to play. Optional bonus videos, only when you tap, for example to double a test-flight bonus. Optional one-time purchases:

  • Remove Ads ($5.99): skip the videos and get every bonus instantly
  • Desk Style Kit ($2.99): new desk styles and a gold stick

No subscription. Purchases from the earlier version of Pointy Stick restore Remove Ads.

Help and FAQ

Where is my progress?

Only on your iPhone. No account or server. Deleting the game deletes progress.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the shop and tap Restore with the same Apple Account.

Are the companies real?

No. Everything in the game is fictional.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
