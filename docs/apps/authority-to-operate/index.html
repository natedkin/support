Authority to Operate

Keep one system approved to run, a shift at a time.

Games · iPhone · Version 1.0

You're the new security officer on a test lab network. Work the ticket board, answer the people in chat, hunt through logs, and write the package the authorizing official has to sign. Honest calls pay off later; shortcuts come back as tickets.

In the game

  • A short opening story: your background, a job board, a recruiter call, a panel interview and your first offer
  • Shifts that run 08:00 to 17:00 with a standup, a live board, chat, email and interruptions
  • Two teammates, Noor and Wes, who take hand-offs and sometimes cut corners you can catch
  • Log hunts and scan findings with a timer
  • A security plan and a fix list the authorizing official marks up in red until she stamps it
  • A career that carries over week to week, with access requests that take real days
  • Assisted mode (the clock stops while you work a ticket) or Realistic mode (it never stops)

iPhone Duo

Open it like a book and the desk splits at the fold: the board stays live on the left and any ticket you open shows on the right, like a second monitor. Half folded on a table, the board stands up and chat lies flat. On the outer screen it plays like a phone.

Pricing

Free to play from Junior ISSO to ISSO, with no time limit.

Senior ISSO (Pro) is a one-time purchase that opens three bigger systems, admin rights and the Senior ISSO promotion. It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account and is shared with Family Sharing. No subscription and no ads.

Help and FAQ

Is any of this real?

No. The companies, people, systems and programs are made up. The ideas come from public security guidance, and nothing in it is a real organization's procedure.

Why is my access request still waiting?

Bigger access takes real days on purpose, the way background checks do. Keep playing shifts; the request moves on its own and shows up in your week review.

Does my career carry to my iPad or Mac?

Yes. It follows your Apple Account through iCloud. The device that saved last wins, so finish a shift before you switch.

How do I restore Senior ISSO?

Open Settings (the gear next to End shift) and tap Restore on the Senior ISSO card, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I turn off the office noise?

Yes. Settings has an Office sounds slider; all the way left is off. Sound effects have their own switch.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
