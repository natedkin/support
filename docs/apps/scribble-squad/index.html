Scribble Squad: Draw Puzzle

Draw the way. Save the squad.

Puzzle game · iPhone · Version 1.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Five doodle kids march out of a hatch and they won't stop walking. Every line you draw with your limited ink turns into solid ground, so you draw bridges, ramps and walls to get them to the door. Steep lines are walls, big drops knock walkers out, and you can keep drawing while they're on the move.

What's inside

  • 100 puzzles in 10 chapters, from Sketchbook to Final Exam
  • Springs, fans, belts, saws, no-draw zones, short pencils and two start boxes
  • A daily puzzle with a bigger squad and a streak to keep
  • A guided first level, and hints that show a dotted outline of one way through
  • Plays offline, no account

iPhone Duo

Open it up and the level sits on one page while the other keeps Pip's note for the level, a live count of who's walking, home and lost, Hint and Retry with your lines, and the chapter map. Half folded on a table, the page stands up and the controls lie flat.

Pricing

Free to play. Optional reward videos for an extra hint when you run out, or to retry a level with 50% more ink, never forced. No banners and no ads between levels.

Optional purchases:

  • Remove Ads (extra hints and the bonus ink retry with no video)
  • 10 Hints

No subscription. Every level can be finished without buying anything.

Help and FAQ

Why did my walkers get knocked out?

They fell too far. Draw a gentle ramp or slide down instead of a sheer drop.

Why do walkers turn around?

Steep lines count as walls. Walkers turn back when they bump one, so keep bridges and ramps gentle.

How do I see the first-level lesson again?

Settings, then Replay tutorial. It plays next time you open level 1.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Remove Ads card, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Found a bug?

Email support and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
