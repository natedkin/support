Full Send Aerospace

Build it. Fly it. Stick the landing.

Simulation game · iPhone · Version 2.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Build a rocket, fly it to orbit with your own thumbs, then land the booster on a ship at sea and fly it again on the next job. About a minute to build, a minute to fly, and a landing that can go beautifully or hilariously wrong.

Highlights

  • Stack it: pick a booster, upper stage, strap-on pods and landing legs for each contract, with live gauges for lift, reach and cost
  • Fly it: throttle, lean onto the ribbon, stage in the green and cut the engine on the orbit you were paid for
  • Stick it: touch down on a rolling ship, the Moon or Mars
  • 7 chapters from a suborbital hop to Mars, 25 payloads, 30 R&D upgrades, 3 rocket families, 74 mission patches
  • Your hangar: reflown boosters with flight counts, wear and nicknames
  • Your own space station with 10 modules, cargo runs and docking by hand
  • Daily Launch Window and weekly events
  • On a folding iPhone, half-fold it for a Mission Control console

Pricing

Free to play. Optional reward videos give bonuses, up to 10 a day, never forced, no banners. Optional purchases:

  • Stardust packs and Legacy Medal packs
  • Ad Tickets
  • Starter and Founders packs
  • Remove Ads (every bonus without a video)

No subscription.

Help and FAQ

Where is my progress?

Only on your iPhone. No account or server. Deleting the game deletes progress.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore Purchases in the store with the same Apple Account.

Are the companies real?

No. Full Send Aerospace is a parody; all companies, people and products are fictional.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
