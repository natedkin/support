Going Up! Elevator Puzzle

Last on, first off.

Puzzle game · iPhone · Version 1.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

You run the elevator in a busy building. Riders stand in the car in the order they board, and whoever got on last is right by the door. The car stops at every floor from the bottom up, and the rider at the door steps out at their floor, until someone headed higher is in the way. Load it right and everybody gets home in one smooth trip.

What's inside

  • 60 buildings across Maple Street, Harbor Heights, Velvet Tower, Pairview Plaza and Skyline One
  • A guided first building that walks you through boarding, the bench and GO
  • VIPs in top hats who always stand at the door, and twins who take two spots
  • No timers and no lives: undo any move before you send the car
  • Plays offline, no account

iPhone Duo

Open it up and the building sits on one page while the other shows a floor board and a live preview of who gets out on which floor if you send the car right now. Half folded on a table, the building stands up and the lobby, bench and keys lie flat.

Help and FAQ

Why did nobody get out?

The rider at the door was going higher than the floor the car stopped at, so everyone behind them rode back down. Put the lowest floor at the door before you tap GO.

How do I use the bench?

Tap the rider standing at the car door to sit them on the bench. Tap a benched rider to board them again. They step in at the door.

How do I see the first-building guide again?

Settings, then Replay the first-building guide. It plays next time you open Maple Street 1.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore Purchases in the Shop or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.
