Unhabit

Quit habits and track recovery, without the all-or-nothing reset.

Health & Fitness · iPhone · Version 1.1.0

View on the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Most habit trackers treat one slip like a full reset. Unhabit helps you recover quickly, understand your triggers and keep moving forward. Log cravings in seconds, record slips without losing your journey, and watch your Recovery Score grow.

Highlights

  • Log cravings and slips in seconds
  • Recovery Score instead of fragile streaks
  • Milestones, patterns and triggers
  • Export your recovery report
  • Private and local-first: your logs stay on your iPhone

Pricing

Unhabit is free and tracks two unhabits at once. Unhabit Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads. Pro adds:

  • Track every unhabit at once
  • Streak history and advanced analytics
  • All recovery insights and full weekly reviews
  • The full milestone path
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets
  • Extra looks with matching app icons

Earlier Unhabit Premium purchases (yearly or Lifetime) keep everything.

Help and FAQ

How do I restore Pro?

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I had Premium. Do I need Pro?

No. Earlier Premium purchases unlock everything Pro does.

How do I cancel an old Premium yearly subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your logs are stored on your device and never sent to us.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
