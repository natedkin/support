Potion Pour

Sort the colors. Brew new potions.

Puzzle · iPhone · Version 1.0

Coming soon to the App StorePrivacy PolicyEmail support

Potion Pour is a cozy color-sort puzzle. Sort each vial into a single pure color. On brew levels, pour two colors into the Cauldron to mix a brand-new potion and fill the order.

Highlights

  • 50 hand-tuned levels, every one solvable
  • The Cauldron: brew new colors from primaries
  • A Brew Lab and Potion Grimoire with 9 potions to discover
  • Earn 3 stars on every level and unlock new lab themes
  • Power-ups when you're stuck: Hint, Undo, Add-Vial and the Magic Wand
  • No timers, no lives. Plays offline

Pricing

Free with ads between levels and optional reward videos for power-ups.

  • Alchemist Pass (one-time): removes ads between levels and unlocks every theme
  • Starter Kit (one-time)
  • Coin packs and the Piggy Bank (used up when spent)

No subscription.

Help and FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Coin packs and the Piggy Bank are used up and aren't restored.

Can I play without personalized ads?

Yes. Decline tracking and you still play every level, with non-personalized ads.

Where is my progress?

On your device. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I contact support?

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.
