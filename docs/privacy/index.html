Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Each app by Nathan Edkin has its own privacy policy, listed below. That page is the one that applies to the app you use.

In general

  • No accounts and no servers run by us. Your data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account where an app says so.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't run our own analytics.
  • Some games and apps show ads through Google AdMob. Where required by law, Google's consent form is shown first, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you allow or decline tracking. Declining never removes features.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Our apps are not directed to children under 13.
