Scribble Squad: Draw Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Scribble Squad: Draw Puzzle is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Scribble Squad: Draw Puzzle.

Information we collect

None directly. No account, login or server run by us. Your progress, hints and settings stay on your device.

Advertising and tracking

Optional reward videos play only when you choose one (an extra hint when you run out, or a retry with 50% more ink after a miss). There are no banners and no ads between levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice under Ad Privacy Choices in Settings.

On first launch the game asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, every feature still works and AdMob shows non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. Remove Ads gives every reward with no video.

Purchases

Optional purchases: Remove Ads (one-time) and 10 Hints. No subscription. Apple processes all payments; we never see your payment details.

Permissions

The only permission the game asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Your control

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Settings also has Reset Progress.

Children

Not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
