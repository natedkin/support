Fitness Test Pro Elite Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Fitness Test Pro Elite is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Fitness Test Pro Elite.

Information we collect

None directly. There is no account, login or server run by us. The scores, measurements and results you enter stay on your device. We cannot see them.

Advertising and tracking

The free version shows ads on result screens (a banner and occasional full-screen ads). Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under Ad Privacy Choices in the app.

On first launch the app asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, you still get every feature and AdMob shows non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. Any purchase removes ads.

Purchases

Optional one-time purchases unlock the Full Version for a branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, SOF screening) or All Branches. There is no subscription. Apple processes all payments through the App Store; we never see your payment details. Apple confirms your purchases on your device so they can unlock and restore.

iCloud

Not used. Your results are not synced anywhere.

Permissions

The only permission the app asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. It does not access Apple Health, location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

What we don't collect

No name, email, contacts or analytics run by us.

Your control

Delete saved results in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not affiliated

Fitness Test Pro Elite is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any military branch, the Department of Defense, the Department of Homeland Security or any government agency.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
