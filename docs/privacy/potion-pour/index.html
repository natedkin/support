Potion Pour Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Potion Pour is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Potion Pour.

Information we collect

None directly. Potion Pour has no account, no login and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own analytics.

On your device

Your game progress, coin balance, unlocked themes and settings are stored on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising and tracking

Potion Pour shows ads through Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and optional reward videos you can choose to watch for a power-up. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under Ad Privacy Choices in Settings.

On first launch the app asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, AdMob serves only non-personalized ads and the game plays exactly the same.

AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. The Alchemist Pass removes ads between levels. Ads are set to a family-friendly maximum content rating.

Purchases

The Alchemist Pass and Starter Kit (one-time), plus coin packs and the Piggy Bank (consumable). Apple processes all payments through the App Store; we never see or store your payment details. Restore Purchases uses your Apple Account through Apple's servers.

iCloud

Not used.

Permissions

The only permission the game asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Children

The app is rated 4+ but is not in the Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

Apple (App Store purchases) and Google AdMob (ads), each under its own privacy policy. These are the only third parties involved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
