Authority to Operate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Authority to Operate is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Authority to Operate.

At a glance
Account requiredNo
AdsNone
Tracking permission promptNo
Google consent form (where required)Not applicable
In-app purchasesSenior ISSO (one-time)
SubscriptionNo
iCloud syncYes, your own iCloud
Data sent to usNone

What the game keeps

Authority to Operate saves your career (name you typed, the look you picked, weeks played, trust, access and records) and your settings (sound, office sounds, haptics). There's no account and no server of ours.

iCloud

Your career is also copied to iCloud key-value storage under your own Apple Account so it shows up on your other iPhone, iPad or Mac. Apple stores it; we can't see it. Sign out of iCloud or turn off iCloud for the app and it stays on that device only.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't collect contacts, location, identifiers or usage data.

Purchases

Senior ISSO is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.
