Full Send Aerospace Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Full Send Aerospace is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Full Send Aerospace.

Information we collect

None directly. The game has no accounts, logins or sign-ups, and we run no analytics or servers.

On your device

Your progress, settings and records, including Daily Launch Window scores, stay on your device. We cannot see them, and deleting the game removes them.

Advertising and tracking

Optional reward videos come through Google AdMob, up to 10 a day. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's MORE menu under ADS & PRIVACY.

The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not use your advertising identifier. AdMob may collect device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. Remove Ads, the Founders Pack or an Ad Ticket skips the video for a bonus.

Purchases

All purchases are optional and processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. There is no subscription.

iCloud

Not used.

Permissions

None. No notifications, and no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

Not directed to children under 13 and not in the Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

Apple (App Store purchases) and Google AdMob (reward videos), each under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
