Jumpin Ninja! Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Jumpin Ninja! is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Jumpin Ninja!.

Information we collect

None directly. No account, login or server run by us.

Advertising and tracking

Optional reward videos play only when you choose one in the Market. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice under Ad Privacy Choices.

On first launch the game asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, every feature still works and AdMob shows non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. Dojo Patron gives reward bonuses with no video.

Purchases

Optional one-time purchases: Ninja Pack, Starter Bundle, Shuriken Doubler, Dojo Patron, Storm Clan and Blossom Clan. No subscription. Apple processes all payments; we never see your payment details.

iCloud

Your best score, shuriken, Dojo, unlocks, missions and settings are stored on your device and mirrored to your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so a reinstall or new iPhone keeps your progress. This uses Apple's iCloud under your Apple Account; we cannot see it. Turn off iCloud for the app in iPhone Settings to keep it on this device only.

Permissions

The only permission the game asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Your control

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
