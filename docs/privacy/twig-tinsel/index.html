Twig & Tinsel: Christmas Tree Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Twig & Tinsel: Christmas Tree is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Twig & Tinsel: Christmas Tree.

At a glance
Account requiredNo
AdsNone
Tracking permission promptNo
Google consent form (where required)Not applicable
In-app purchasesFull Tree (one-time)
SubscriptionNo
iCloud syncNo
Data sent to usNone

Your data stays on your device

Twig & Tinsel has no accounts and no server run by us. Your progress (stars, finished trees, saved trees, which Advent pieces you have opened) and your settings (sound, music, voices, captions, haptics) are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising and tracking

No ads, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or usage analytics. The game doesn't need the internet to play.

Purchases

The optional Full Tree is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes all payments through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. Apple confirms what you own on your device so the Full Tree can unlock and restore. Apple's handling is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

iCloud

Not used. Your progress is not synced to any cloud service.

Photos

Photo cards and twinkle clips are shared through the iOS share sheet and go only where you send them. If you pick Save Image there, iOS asks once for permission to add it to your photo library. The app never reads your photos.

Permissions

Add to Photos, only if you save a photo card from the share sheet. Twig & Tinsel does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications or Apple Health.

Your control

Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Children

Twig & Tinsel collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
