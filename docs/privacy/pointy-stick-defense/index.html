Pointy Stick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pointy Stick is made by Nathan Edkin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Pointy Stick.

At a glance
Account requiredNo
AdsOptional bonus videos only
Tracking permission promptYes, optional
Google consent form (where required)Yes
In-app purchasesRemove Ads, Desk Style Kit
SubscriptionNo
iCloud syncNo
Data sent to usNone

Information we collect

None directly. No account, login or sign-up, and we run no servers or analytics. The game never asks for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

On your device

Your office, cash, unlocks, purchases owned and settings (music, sound, haptics) are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Deleting the game removes them.

Advertising and tracking

Optional bonus videos play only when you tap a bonus. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under Ad Privacy Choices in the shop menu.

The game then asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, gameplay is the same and AdMob shows non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this data. Remove Ads skips the videos and gives bonuses instantly.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the Desk Style Kit are optional one-time purchases. No subscription. Apple processes all payments through the App Store; we never see your payment details. Restore uses your Apple Account through Apple's servers.

iCloud

Not used.

Permissions

The only permission the game asks for is App Tracking Transparency, described above. No notifications, and no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

Not directed to children under 13 and not in the Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

Apple (App Store purchases) and Google AdMob (bonus videos), each under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
