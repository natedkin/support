Contact and support

Email natedkinsupport@gmail.com for questions, bug reports, feedback and privacy requests. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days.

Help us help you

  • The app name and version (shown on the App Store page or in the app's Settings or About screen)
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if you can

Purchases

  • Restore: open the app's shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account.
  • Subscriptions: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all payments. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
